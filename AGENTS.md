# Codex collaboration guidance

Read the [research scope](docs/RESEARCH_CONTEXT.md), [model specification](docs/MODEL_SPEC.md), [rule register](docs/MODEL_ASSUMPTIONS.md) and [verification plan](docs/VERIFICATION_PLAN.md) before proposing changes. Check the [progress record](PROJECT_PROGRESS.md) before reporting implementation or results.

PSP305 is an assessed final-year project. The student independently owns the assessed simulation, flow mechanisms, queue scheduling algorithms, experiments, technical analysis and conclusions. Codex can explain concepts, review specifications and code, help diagnose issues and suggest verification approaches. Do not fabricate data, literature, results or test passes. Distinguish student acceptance from assistant-supported checks.

Explain the impact and relevant checks before changing source or experimental design. Keep changes small and preserve approved fixtures, test expectations and research decisions unless the student approves a change. Record rule changes with their rationale and evidence status in the [rule register](docs/MODEL_ASSUMPTIONS.md#10-update-procedure).
